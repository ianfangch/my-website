Visible checkpoints throughout the project

From Brief to Delivery

Quality begins with clear requirements. Each stage creates a practical checkpoint for specifications, samples, production progress, inspection findings and shipment preparation.

01

Requirement Review

We review product lists, drawings, dimensions, target market and delivery expectations before sourcing begins.

02

Specification Alignment

Materials, finishes, hardware, packaging and tolerances are clarified so quotations can be compared accurately.

03

Sample Approval

Samples, finish boards or prototypes provide a clear reference before bulk production is confirmed.

04

Production Follow-up

Key milestones are followed and progress updates help surface questions before they affect the delivery plan.

05

Inspection

Products are checked against the agreed specification, with findings documented for review and corrective action.

06

Packing & Delivery

Export packing, marks and shipment details are coordinated for safer handling and a more organized arrival.

Commercial planning

What Is Confirmed Before Production

MOQ and production timing vary by product, construction, finish, quantity and packaging. They are confirmed in the quotation after the project requirements are reviewed.

No generic promise. Final specifications, commercial terms and the production schedule are recorded in the approved quotation or order documents.

MOQ

Confirmed according to the selected product, finish and order combination.

Lead Time

Confirmed after specifications, quantity, approvals and packaging requirements are agreed.

Order Reference

Models, dimensions, colours, accessories and quantities are checked against approved documents.

Destination

Delivery destination and shipment requirements are reviewed before the final quotation.

Product-specific checkpoints

What We Review by Category

Cabinetry

  • Approved layout and cabinet dimensions
  • Door material, colour and finish
  • Hardware and internal accessories
  • Package marks for room or unit identification

Kitchen Sinks

  • Model, bowl dimensions and mounting type
  • Material, thickness and surface finish
  • Drain, fittings and included accessories
  • Surface and package protection

Quartz Countertops

  • Colour, thickness and approved fabrication drawing
  • Cut-outs, edge details and finished dimensions
  • Visible surface condition before packing
  • Piece identification and protective packing

Flooring

  • Colour, pattern, construction and thickness
  • Quantity and package-unit information
  • Batch consistency and visible surface review
  • Carton marks and pallet preparation where applicable

Pre-shipment review

Inspection and Packing Checkpoints

The applicable checklist is aligned with the approved product and order requirements. Findings that require attention are reviewed before shipment preparation continues.

  • Model and quantity
  • Dimensions and construction
  • Colour and visible finish
  • Hardware or included accessories
  • Packaging condition and marks
  • Loading quantity and shipment references
01

Packing

Protection and package identification are reviewed according to the product and agreed order requirements.

02

Loading

Shipment references and loading quantity are checked against the prepared packing information.

03

Documents

Commercial and packing information is prepared according to the confirmed shipment arrangement.
