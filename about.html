Our story

From Sourcing to Whole-Home Supply

IanProject grew from hands-on product sourcing and manufacturing coordination into a China-based supply partner for international home and interior projects. Our capabilities now extend across cabinetry, furniture, sinks, surfaces, hardware and related home products.

We support distributors, contractors, designers, developers, project buyers and individual homeowners, serving international clients with flexible solutions for projects and orders of different types and scales.
