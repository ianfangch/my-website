Quality planning

Pre-Production Approval Checklist

Create one approved reference for what will be produced, checked, packed and identified.

Confirm the product reference

Review model names, quantities, dimensions and drawings. Revisions should be identified clearly so production does not rely on an outdated reference.

Approve materials and finishes

Record material construction, colour, surface finish, hardware and accessories. Physical samples or finish references may be necessary when screen colours are not sufficient.

Review packaging and identification

Confirm what requires individual protection, carton or crate identification, room or unit marks, and any project-specific packing information.

Define inspection points

  • Model and quantity
  • Dimensions and construction
  • Colour and visible finish
  • Hardware and included accessories
  • Packaging and marks

Release production against approved information

The production schedule should be confirmed only after the required approvals, quantity and commercial documents are aligned.

Keep one approval record

Use an approved quotation, drawing set, specification sheet or sample reference as the shared basis for production and inspection.
