Product planning

Standard vs Custom Products

Choose where standardisation saves time and where customisation is necessary for the project.

When standard products help

Standard models provide an existing reference for dimensions, construction and appearance. They can make early selection and quotation comparison more direct, especially when the project can accept available specifications.

When custom products are appropriate

Customisation is useful when dimensions, layouts, finishes, functions or coordinated materials must follow a project design. It requires clearer drawings, approvals and production references.

Compare the decision points

  • Required dimensions and site constraints
  • Available versus project-specific finishes
  • Quantity and repeatability
  • Sample, drawing and prototype approvals
  • Packaging, identification and installation coordination

A mixed approach

Many projects combine standard products with selected custom elements. This can preserve design priorities while reducing unnecessary custom work.

Before requesting a quotation

Mark each requirement as fixed, preferred or flexible. This gives the supplier a clearer basis for proposing standard or customised options.
