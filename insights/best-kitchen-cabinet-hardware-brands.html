Global hardware guide

Best Kitchen Cabinet Hardware Brands

How to choose hinges, drawer systems, lift mechanisms and storage fittings—and which global brands are strongest in each category.

Updated August 24, 2026  ·  12-minute read

Good cabinet hardware should feel almost invisible: doors stay aligned, drawers run quietly and storage remains easy to use after years of daily work. The best specification is not simply the most expensive brand; it is the correct product family, load rating, corrosion resistance and after-sales support for the project.

Editorial note: this is an independent buyer’s shortlist, not a sponsored ranking. Product ranges and distribution differ by country. Confirm current specifications, warranties, authorised supply and replacement-part availability before ordering.

Recommended hardware combinations

Project positionSuggested approach
Premium residentialUse Blum or Hettich for hinges, drawers and lift systems; select the exact series according to front weight and drawer load.
Balanced mid-marketCombine selected Hettich, Salice or Häfele ranges with a clearly approved model schedule.
Large developmentUse tested DTC ranges for volume supply, with Blum, Hettich, Salice or Häfele retained at high-use or specification-critical points.

Do not approve a quotation that says only “Blum or equivalent.” Ask for the brand, series, model number, finish, load rating, opening angle and soft-close or push-open function. Keep one signed sample set and include substitution approval in the purchase contract.
