Before you request a quotation

Send every shortlisted supplier the same information: project location, drawings, quantities, cabinet construction, board and finish requirements, hardware schedule, countertop scope, packing method, installation responsibility and required delivery date. “Nearby port” above is a logistics reference only—the actual loading port depends on the factory, Incoterm and shipping route.
