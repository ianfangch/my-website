Sourcing guide

How to Prepare a Product Enquiry

A clear brief helps the supplier understand what must be priced, confirmed and delivered.

Start with the project context

State the project type, destination market and intended application. This helps separate a retail request from a residential development, hotel, commercial project or distribution enquiry.

Identify products and quantities

List the required product category, model or reference image, estimated quantity and unit of measure. If quantities are preliminary, mark them as estimates instead of presenting them as final.

Define specifications

  • Dimensions, layout or drawings
  • Material, construction and thickness
  • Colour, finish and hardware
  • Accessories or components to include
  • Packaging or labelling requirements

Include delivery information

Provide the destination city, port or country and the target delivery period. Production and shipment planning can only be confirmed after the specification, quantity and destination are reviewed.

Enquiry checklist

Project type · product/model · quantity · dimensions · finish · accessories · packaging · destination · target date · inspection requirement.
