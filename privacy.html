Privacy Policy

How information sent through IanProject’s communication channels is handled.

Information You Choose to Send

IanProject may receive information that you voluntarily send by email, WhatsApp or other linked communication channels. This may include your name, company, contact details, project location, drawings, product lists, quantities, specifications and reference images.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, review project requirements, prepare quotations, coordinate samples or production, provide delivery support and maintain relevant business records.

Service Providers and Project Partners

Relevant project information may be shared with suitable suppliers, logistics providers or professional service providers when needed to review or fulfil your request. Information is limited to what is reasonably required for that purpose.

Third-Party Platforms

This website links to email, WhatsApp, YouTube and social-media platforms. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services. IanProject does not control third-party websites or applications.

Website Analytics

IanProject uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve site content. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source and interactions with the website using cookies or similar technologies. This information is reviewed in aggregated reports and is not used by IanProject to identify visitors personally. You can restrict analytics cookies through your browser settings. Google processes analytics information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and Security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for enquiries, project coordination, legal obligations and business records. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Request

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. Contact info@ianproject.com.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when website features or business processes change. Last updated: August 2026.
